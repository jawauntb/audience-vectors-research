## Study Title

Short video memorability judgment study

## Short Description

You will watch pairs of short generated videos and choose which one you think
would be more memorable. The study is about media perception and should take
about 8-12 minutes.

## Participant-Facing Description

In this study, you will see a series of short video pairs. Each pair was created
from the same prompt. For each pair, please choose the video that you think would
be more memorable if you saw many similar clips.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your judgment of
memorability and attention. Please use a desktop or laptop browser with stable
internet and keep the survey tab open until completion.

## Inclusion Criteria

- Age 18 or older
- Fluent English reader
- Desktop or laptop browser
- Normal or corrected-to-normal vision
- Comfortable watching short generated videos

## Exclusion / Quality Criteria

- Duplicate participation
- Failed attention checks
- Extremely fast completion inconsistent with watching the clips
- Incomplete submission
- Technical failure that prevents video playback

## Estimated Time

8-12 minutes for 24 pairwise trials.
