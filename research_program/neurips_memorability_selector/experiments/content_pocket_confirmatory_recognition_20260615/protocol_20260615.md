# Confirmatory Multi-Pocket Recognition Protocol

Date: 2026-06-15

## Status

- Status: `protocol_ready_for_generation_adapter`
- Human data: none
- Generated videos for this study: none
- Claim level: scaffold

## Research Question

Can compute selectors prospectively choose generated videos that humans
recognize better after a delay, across multiple content families and after
controlling for quality, motion, low-level salience, and content-family effects?

## Discovery-Regime Audit

Old regime:

- Artifact schema: SVD replay clips, seed images, prompts, TRIBE replay scores,
  exact V-JEPA features, CLIP diagnostics, visual-gate statuses, pocket labels,
  Prolific delayed recognition responses.
- Main human gate: pooled orange-flowers/hanging-clothes old-vs-lure recognition
  exceeded hard controls in Wave 2.
- Allowed claim: narrow delayed recognition evidence for one primary
  content-pocket packet.

Transition:

- New generator family: fresh Seedance 2.0 videos rather than only SVD replay.
- New operation: prospective selector assignment within content families.
- New gate: mixed-effects delayed recognition across many families, with
  released stimulus bundle and durable response capture.

Transported evidence:

- Orange flowers and hanging clothes remain anchor families.
- Blue jellyfish and old car remain boundary families.
- Aerial beach, city street, and storm beach remain hard-control-style families.
- Complete-candidate retention, visual screening, V-JEPA caveats, and
  participant-safe aggregate outputs are preserved.

Rejected alternatives:

- Do not broaden the claim by only writing a larger narrative paper.
- Do not run another preference-only study as the primary endpoint.
- Do not use webhook.site as the durable collector.
- Do not generate beautiful videos before the selection and release schema are
  frozen.

Residual finding sought:

- Whether compute selectors predict human delayed recognition beyond content
  category and low-level visual descriptors.

## Stimulus Design

The study uses 12 content families. Each family receives a candidate generation
pool before human data:

- 8 candidate old videos per family.
- 1 selected `selector_top` old video per family.
- 1 selected `quality_matched_control` old video per family.
- 2 same-category lure attempts per selected old video, with one accepted lure
  and one backup where screening permits.
- 24 unrelated filler old/lure pairs.

The selected launch set therefore contains:

- 24 analysis old videos.
- 24 analysis lures.
- 24 filler old videos.
- 24 filler lures.

Videos should be audio-free, 4-6 seconds, same duration, same resolution, same
fps, no text, no watermark, and no obvious generated-video failure.

## Content Families

The initial family set intentionally mixes known positives, previous controls,
boundary pockets, and new visually diverse categories:

- orange flowers
- hanging clothes
- blue jellyfish
- old car
- aerial beach
- city street
- storm beach
- butterflies on flowers
- street food grill
- rain on window
- hands on pottery wheel
- candle flame on table

The first seven preserve continuity with the accepted pocket regime. The final
five expand the benchmark so the result is not just an orange/clothes story.

## Participant Design

Use 8 counterbalanced forms. Each participant sees one analysis item per content
family, not both the selector-top and matched-control item from the same family.
Each form has 6 selector-top and 6 matched-control analysis items, plus 12
unrelated fillers.

Session 1:

- brief consent/instructions;
- 12 analysis old videos, one per content family;
- 12 unrelated filler old videos;
- simple cover task after each clip.

Session 2:

- delayed old-vs-lure 2AFC recognition for the 12 analysis items;
- confidence rating for each recognition choice;
- optional post-choice free-text recall;
- 12 filler recognition trials;
- media-load/error flags retained per trial.

## Cohort Size

Use these thresholds:

- Minimum interpretable delayed sample: 300 usable Session 2 participants.
- Target delayed sample: 400 usable Session 2 participants.
- Stretch delayed sample: 500 usable Session 2 participants.

With expected 85% retention, recruit approximately:

- 353 Session 1 participants for 300 delayed usable participants.
- 471 Session 1 participants for 400 delayed usable participants.
- 589 Session 1 participants for 500 delayed usable participants.

Recommendation: target 400 usable delayed participants and allow a stretch
top-up to 500 if retention and budget are healthy.

## Primary Gate

The primary gate passes only if:

- the manifest is frozen before human launch;
- all selected analysis videos and lures pass visual screening;
- durable collector exports complete Session 1 and Session 2 payloads;
- `selector_top` recognition exceeds `quality_matched_control` recognition in a
  mixed-effects logistic model;
- the effect remains positive under leave-one-family-out sensitivity;
- quality, motion, colorfulness, saliency, and CLIP/V-JEPA similarity covariates
  do not collapse the selector effect to zero.

## Claim Boundary

Allowed if the gate passes:

- Prospectively selected generated videos are recognized better after delay than
  matched controls in this Seedance multi-pocket benchmark.
- Compute selector scores contain human-recognition-relevant signal beyond a
  simple category-only story.

Not allowed without further evidence:

- Human memorability is solved.
- Generated videos are broadly more memorable in every setting.
- TRIBE/V-JEPA/CLIP scores are themselves human memory.
- Prompting or generator steering caused the effect unless independently tested.
- The result is measured-fMRI/BMD validation.
