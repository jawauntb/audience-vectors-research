# Consent Form Draft

This is draft language for faculty/IRB review.

## Study Title

Brain-Aligned Memorability Signals for Generated Video Selection

## Invitation

You are invited to participate in an online research study about how people judge
the memorability of short videos. Participation is voluntary.

## What You Will Do

You will view pairs of short videos and answer which video you think would be
more memorable. Some videos may be generated by artificial intelligence systems.
The study may include attention-check questions to make sure the task is working
properly.

## Time

The study is expected to take approximately 8-15 minutes.

## Risks

The risks are minimal. You may experience boredom, fatigue, or mild visual
discomfort from watching short videos. Some videos may include motion or rapid
visual changes. You may stop participating at any time.

## Benefits

You may not directly benefit from participating. Your responses may help
researchers understand how to evaluate generated videos and predict which videos
people find memorable.

## Privacy

The study will collect your task responses and may collect your Prolific
participant ID for payment and duplicate prevention. The research team will not
ask for your name, email address, phone number, medical history, or other
sensitive personal information. Results will be reported in aggregate or using
de-identified data.

## Voluntary Participation

Your participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time. If you stop before
completing the study, your compensation may depend on Prolific and study-team
policies described in the final study listing.
