# Handoff — 2026-05-18 session

Summary of what changed in this session, for the other agent.

## TL;DR

The framework gained external grounding:

1. **Prolific human validation landed.** n=41 raters, 100% attention-check pass.
   **290/451 = 64.3% of human responses prefer the TRIBE-projection-ranked
   best-of-N winner** over a within-seed median variant (Wilson 95% CI
   [0.598, 0.686], binomial p = 1.3 × 10⁻⁹). This closes the previously open
   "Best-of-N is a metric-internal production hypothesis" §8 limitation.

2. **Reviewer-corrected audience-decomposition claim.** Per-persona signed
   cosine mean +0.02 was a bimodal cancellation artifact, not orthogonality.
   The right metric is |cos| = **0.434** and entropy-based effective rank =
   **3.56 / 12**. The 12 Haiku personas span ~4 latent axes, not 12 distinct
   ones. Sign-flipped persona vectors share an axis with opposite "memorable"
   polarity.

3. **α-steering claim made honest.** Multi-seed α=±5 mean ρ = +0.22 is *not*
   statistically significant against zero (t-test p = 0.44, Wilcoxon p = 0.41).
   Only the 1-seed α=±10 result is real. Disclosed in abstract, §6.6, §8.

4. **Ablation interpretation strengthened.** Added explicit prose in §5.3.5
   tying together the three controls that defeat the "by construction" critique:
   nonlinear probes (§5.3.3, RF drops 0.41 → −0.03), multi-direction extraction
   (§5.3.5, 9 additional orthogonal directions all give ρ ≈ −0.045), and
   ICA (§5.3.6.1, best unsupervised component |ρ| = 0.14, ~3× weaker than
   supervised).

5. **Survey infrastructure shipped + live.** Survey hosted on Cloudflare Pages,
   anonymous webhook response collector, automated polling, analysis script,
   all checked in. Prolific study completed in ~1.5 hours.
